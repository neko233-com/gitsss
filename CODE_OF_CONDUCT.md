# Gitsss Community Code of Conduct

Gitsss welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities,
experiences, and communication styles. We expect everyone to communicate with
respect and to keep project spaces safe and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Discuss code, designs, and behavior without personal attacks.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private information, credentials, and internal repository data.
- Take responsibility for the impact of your words and actions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination,
sexualized attention, publishing private information without consent, and
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to the project maintainers. For security issues,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not use a public issue. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action
when behavior violates this policy.

This policy applies to project repositories, review spaces, internal
collaboration channels, events, and anyone representing Gitsss.

This document is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
